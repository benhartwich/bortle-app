Bortle is a cockpit for astrophotography: planning, capture, quality control, stacking and developing to the finished image. The app puts your Bortle on your phone, whether on bortle.eu or on your own server.

TONIGHT
• The state of the night, running developments with progress and time left
• The session's events, grouped by day: frames, autofocus, plate solves, meridian flips
• Approvals: where Bortle asks before the next step, you answer right there

AT THE TELESCOPE (with N.I.N.A.)
• The camera's latest frame with HFR, star count and guiding
• Every frame of the night and its curves: HFR, stars, guiding, temperature, focus
• Every device on its own: camera, mount, focuser, filter wheel, guiding, rotator and flat panel, with connection status
• Mount: slew to a catalogue target, tracking, park, polar alignment (TPPA), meridian flip
• Framing: turn and shift your field on the sky image, then slew and centre
• A sky map with tonight's targets that follows your phone
• Stop the sequence or end the night through N.I.N.A., always after asking you

PLAN
• Look up an object and see its altitude over your horizon tonight, with the moon and clouds
• Send the ready sequence to N.I.N.A., with the develop level you want

GALLERY
• Finished images and the log of every run, step by step

NOTIFICATIONS
• When an approval waits for you, an image is done or a run failed, and when the night is over (with bortle.eu)

FOR THE NIGHT OUTSIDE
• Red light mode that keeps your dark adaptation

What you need: an account on bortle.eu – Bortle is in private beta, request access on bortle.eu ("Zugang beantragen") – or your own Bortle install, whose address you enter when you sign in. The app controls only a telescope you run yourself. Mount safety (parking, aborts, meridian flips) stays in N.I.N.A.; the app does not interfere with it.

No trackers, no ads. Bortle is open source: github.com/benhartwich/bortle-app
